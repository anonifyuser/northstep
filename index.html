Anatomy of the Arc 01

Four layers. 218 grams.

  • Upper
  • Midsole
  • Plate
  • Outsole

Layer 1 of 4 · 74 g

Upper: one piece of engineered mesh

Knit in a single pass, so there are no seams across the toes to rub. A denser weave through the midfoot holds the laces, and an open weave over the forefoot lets heat out.

Layer 2 of 4 · 96 g

Midsole: foam that gives back

36 mm of stack at the heel and 30 mm at the forefoot. The foam returns most of the energy you put into it, and the wide base keeps every landing steady.

Layer 3 of 4 · 12 g

Plate: a spring, not a blade

A thin nylon plate set to flex under the ball of your foot, so toe-off feels like a push instead of a stiff board.

Layer 4 of 4 · 36 g

Outsole: rubber only where you land

Hard rubber at the heel and under the ball of the foot. The rest is left bare to save weight, and it can be resoled when the rubber wears through.

All four together

74 + 96 + 12 + 36 = 218 g

That is the whole shoe, in a US men's 9.
